Rooted in Austin, grown with purpose

Summit Ridge was founded on a simple idea: outdoor spaces should be as intentional and livable as the home they surround.

We work with native Texas plants, sustainable practices, and thoughtful design to build landscapes that thrive in the Hill Country climate — not fight against it. Every project begins with listening to how you actually use your yard, and ends with a space that invites you back outside.
